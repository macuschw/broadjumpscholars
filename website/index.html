The problem

A tape measure says how far. Not why.

  1. 01

    Distance is the only number

    Most testing records one result per jump. Two athletes can land at the same mark with very different technique.

  2. 02

    Lab gear is out of reach

    Force plates and motion-capture systems cost thousands and live in a lab, not on the field where athletes train.

  3. 03

    Biomechanics are invisible

    Launch angle, trunk lean and push-off timing happen in under half a second, too fast for the eye to judge.
