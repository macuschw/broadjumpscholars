FAQ

Are the numbers on this site real?

No. Every number in the demo and on these pages is simulated sample data, labelled as such. Real results will appear once the analysis pipeline is connected.

What equipment do I need?

A phone that records 120–240 fps slow motion, a tripod, and a tape measure for calibration. See the setup guide.

How accurate is it?

It's a single 2D camera, so it's built for consistent field testing rather than lab precision. A validation study against tape-measured jumps is in progress.

Can I use normal 30 fps video?

Yes for distance and angles, but timing is only good to ±33 ms per event. Slow motion (120–240 fps) makes contact and flight times meaningful.

Does my video get uploaded?

Not in this demo. A file you choose stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
