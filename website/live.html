Measure live

Point a phone at the jump

The distance appears, and is said out loud, as soon as the jumper has held the landing. A step, a hand down or a fall is called out instead: that jump doesn't count. Nothing is uploaded while measuring, and the session is recorded, so the full analysis can follow, now or later.

Remote screen: a laptop or a second phone shows the distances and runs the phone on the tripod, so nobody has to touch it, then brings the video and the results over. Open it here, then scan its code with the filming phone.

On a Mac, an iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID can also be this laptop's own camera (Continuity Camera): pick it after Start the camera.

Before you start

A setup that measures well

  • Phone on a tripod, side-on, 4–5 m back
  • A tape measure on the floor, on the camera's side of the feet
  • Toes level with the start of the tape
  • Feet together at takeoff and landing
  • Nobody else in the frame
