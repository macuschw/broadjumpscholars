Before you upload

A video that measures well

  • Phone on a tripod, side-on to the jump
  • Slow motion: 120 or 240 fps
  • A tape measure on the floor, in shot
  • Feet together at takeoff and landing
  • Nobody else in the frame
Full setup guide

Measure live

Point a phone at the jump: the distance appears, and is said out loud, the moment the jumper lands. The session is recorded, so the full analysis can follow, now or later.

Remote screen: a laptop or a second phone shows the distances and runs the phone on the tripod, so nobody has to touch it. Open it here, then scan its code with the filming phone.

On a Mac, an iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID can also be this laptop's own camera (Continuity Camera): pick it after Start the camera.
