How it works

From a phone video to biomechanics in four steps.

No sensors on the athlete and no lab. One phone, side-on, captures everything the analysis needs.

  1. 01

    Record

    Phone on a tripod, side-on to the jump, filming slow motion at 120–240 fps.

    One clip per attempt

  2. 02

    Track

    AI pose tracking finds 33 body landmarks in every frame: hips, knees, ankles, heels, toes.

    MediaPipe Pose

  3. 03

    Calculate

    Physics turns positions into results: a projectile fit for the flight, vectors for angles, frames for timing.

    Six core metrics

  4. 04

    Improve

    Compare attempts side by side to see what changed between them, not just how far each one went.

    Side-by-side comparison
